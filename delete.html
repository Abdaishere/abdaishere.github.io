Delete your data

PolyBall has no accounts, so there is nothing of yours on a server to delete. Everything the game knows about you is on your phone.

The fastest way

Uninstalling PolyBall removes the save file, your settings, your scores and the gameplay log with it. Nothing survives the uninstall and nothing is kept elsewhere.

Without uninstalling

In the game: Settings › DATA › RESET PROGRESS clears your scores and progress. Settings › DATA also turns the gameplay log off and deletes it.

Note that Android may keep a backup of the save in your own Google Drive if you have Android Backup switched on. That copy belongs to your Google account, not to us, and Google's own backup settings control it.

Purchases

Purchase records are held by Google Play, not by us. We never see your name, your email or your payment details. Refunds and purchase history are handled in the Play Store or at play.google.com/store/account.

Ads

Google AdMob may hold an advertising identifier associated with your device. You control it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, where you can delete or reset it. In the EEA and the UK, the game's Settings › DATA › AD CONSENT row reopens the consent form so you can withdraw consent.

Ask us

If you want confirmation in writing, or you believe some data of yours exists that this page does not cover, email abdaishere@gmail.com and you will get an answer within 30 days.
