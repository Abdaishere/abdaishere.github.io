PLAYING FAIR

PolyBall has a public leaderboard and a monthly cup, so fair play is the whole point. Do not use modified clients, scripts, bots, automation, altered timers or anything else that makes a score the game could not have produced by hand. Do not send the server scores the game did not play.

The server checks every score it receives. A score that could not have happened is refused and never appears. A score that passes but trips one of the softer checks is shown, and the server keeps a note of it for up to 90 days so I can look. I can remove any score, or every score from an install, when I believe it was not played fairly, and I can shut an install out of the online features altogether. I decide in good faith on what the server recorded; this is a game, and the board only works if the scores on it are real. If your score goes, you can email me and I will tell you why.

Runs that used a Combo Shield or an ad revive are marked assisted and are never sent to the board. That is by design, so nothing you can buy or watch changes your rank.

YOUR NAME ON THE BOARD

You can pick a name of 2 to 12 characters to show beside your scores. That name appears publicly, in the game and on abdaishere.com, to anyone who looks at the board. Pick one you are happy for strangers to see.

Keep it clean. No slurs, no sexual content, no harassment, no impersonating other people, no advertising, nothing hateful. The server checks names against a blocklist before they show, and any player can hide your name on their own device or report it to me. When enough different players report the same name (three, or five for a player in the top three or in a past cup result; brand-new installs do not count), the board shows a generated name in its place until I have looked at it. If I find a name over the line I replace it with a generated one and keep that decision against your install, so it stays off. Your score stays; only the name goes.

By choosing a name you give me permission to show it on the board, in the game and on the website, for as long as your scores are there.

THE MONTHLY CUP

Each month the board runs as a cup. When the month closes, the standings are frozen into an archive so past results can still be shown, and the top three receive in-game cosmetics: a champion plate and title, a silver plate and a bronze plate. There are no cash prizes and nothing of monetary value. If a winning score fails a later check, I can remove it and move the places below it up. I can change how the cup works, pause it or stop running it. Already-frozen results stay as they were, except where a score is removed under the sentence above.
