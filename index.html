PolyBall

A polygon arena arcade game for Android.

Match the ball to the segment it is about to hit. That is the whole game, and it gets faster than you do.

What it costs

Free, with ads

Interstitials between runs, never during one, and never on your first day. Optional rewarded ads you choose to watch.

Nothing is sold that affects play

Every purchase is cosmetic or removes ads. No item makes the ball easier to steer, and nothing random is ever sold for money.

The daily run stays free

One attempt per day, the same for everyone, and no purchase buys another.

Your data

Saves, scores and settings stay on your device. The gameplay log is off unless you switch it on, and it never leaves the phone unless you export it yourself. The full detail is in the privacy policy, and you can delete everything at any time.
